## Council Responsibilities

The Founding Steering Council is responsible for general tasks associated with a steering council for this project:

- determining roadmap and priorities

The Founding Steering Council is also responsible for the following tasks associated with project start-up:
- formalizing scope of responsibilities for the Steering Council
- clarifying roles for organizations and individuals associated with the project
- identifying long-term operational plans for sustainability
